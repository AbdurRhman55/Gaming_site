Parking Fury 2: New Maps, Same Tight Parking

Parking Fury 2 is the direct sequel to the 2016 original, released on 23 March 2017 by the same developer, Andriy Pidvirnyy. The handling and goal are unchanged — reach the marked space without crashing — but every map is new, and Coolmath Games notes a couple of added features over the first game.

What the official page says

The instructions on Coolmath Games boil the game down to one sentence: “Use WASD or the arrow keys to drive your car! Follow the arrows and stop in the yellow parking space. Be careful not to crash into walls or vehicles!” That is genuinely all there is to learn — the skill comes from the map layouts, not from extra mechanics.

Coolmath classifies the game under Skill → Hard Controls and lists the skills it exercises as planning, visual-spatial reasoning and estimation. Like the first game, it runs in a browser and on mobile devices, with the on-screen steering wheel and pedals layout on touch screens.

ℹ How it differs from the original: Coolmath’s own FAQ describes both sequels as being made by the same developer and playing “very similarly to the first Parking Fury, just with new maps and a couple of new features.” No level count is published for this entry — only the original’s 10 levels are confirmed.

How to approach it

Because the controls are identical to the first game, everything in our controls guide carries over unchanged. The difference is map geometry: sequels squeeze bays into narrower gaps and add obstacles you must thread through first. The reliable pattern is still brake early, straighten the car before the final metre, and enter the yellow space slowly enough that a small steering correction still fits.

If you are stuck on a specific layout, the walkthrough page explains the level structure of the original and a method that transfers to every sequel, and the tips page covers the mistakes that cause most retried runs.

FAQ

Parking Fury 2 questions

Does Parking Fury 2 have the same controls as Parking Fury?
Yes. Both use WASD or the arrow keys on desktop, and the on-screen wheel with gas and brake pedals on touch devices. See the full controls guide.
How many levels does Parking Fury 2 have?
Coolmath Games does not publish a level count for the sequel — only the original’s 10 levels are confirmed. Any other number you see quoted elsewhere is unverified.
Is Parking Fury 2 on mobile?
The official page lists both browser and mobile platforms and includes touch instructions, so yes on the platforms Coolmath supports. Our mobile section explains the layout.
