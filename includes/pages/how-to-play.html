Parking Fury Controls: Keyboard, Touch and Every Version

Parking Fury has almost nothing to memorise — but the controls differ between the top-down games and the 3D series, and the touch layout is not obvious until someone tells you what the pedals do. Everything below is taken from the official game pages, checked in October 2026.

The controls for the original Parking Fury

Coolmath Games’ current instructions for the original are one line: “Use WASD or the arrow keys to drive and turn your car. Drive your car into the highlighted parking space and move on to the next car. Don’t hit the walls or other parked cars!”

Parking Fury on a keyboard (official instructions)

W / ↑ Accelerate forward Short taps near the bay keep the car controllable
S / ↓ Reverse Also your brake — release and tap to settle the car
A D / ← → Turn the car Steering only matters while the car is moving
Mouse / touch Not used in the top-down games The whole game is keyboard or on-screen controls
ℹ There is no separate handbrake or boost in the top-down games. The official instruction sheet lists drive, reverse and steer — nothing else. If a guide tells you to press a special key for the original games, it is describing something else.

Playing on a phone or tablet

The mobile version of the same game swaps the keyboard for on-screen hardware. Coolmath publishes these touch instructions for the series:

  • Steer: rotate the on-screen steering wheel.
  • Go forward: tap the gas pedal.
  • Go backwards: tap the brake pedal.
  • Objective: follow the arrows and park inside the yellow rectangle.

Coolmath also documents installing the game to your home screen so it reopens without hunting for the page: in Safari on iOS, tap the Share icon and choose “Add to Home Screen”; on Android, open the browser menu and choose “Install App”. Their pages list the series as supporting both browser and mobile platforms.

What actually differs in the 3D games

Parking Fury 3D and its sequels are 3D games, so they add the keys a 3D driving game needs: Ctrl to get in and out of vehicles, C to cycle the camera, and Space as a handbrake in Bounty Hunter. Poki lists all three as desktop titles. Full tables live on the Parking Fury 3D page.

How to actually use the controls (a 30-second method)

  1. Line up in a straight line first. Do your braking before the turn so the final approach is a slow, straight glide.
  2. Steer in taps, not holds. A couple of quick presses arc the car into the bay; holding steer from speed overshoots the slot.
  3. Finish at walking pace. The car should practically creep the last few centimetres — that leaves room to correct instead of crashing.
  4. Reset after a scrape. Once you have touched a wall, restart the attempt rather than reversing out of a mess.

It is the same four steps whether you are on keys, a wheel overlay or a 3D camera. More detail: tips and common mistakes and the walkthrough method.

Quick FAQ

Does Parking Fury use the mouse?
In the top-down games, no — driving is entirely keyboard or on-screen controls. The mouse is only used around the site, not in the game.
Can I remap the keys?
None of the official pages publish a key-remapping option, so assume the defaults: WASD or the arrow keys. Both layouts work identically, so pick whichever hand position you prefer.
Why does my car not turn when I press the arrow keys?
Steering needs movement — if the car is stationary or barely crawling, turning input does very little. Roll forward or back a little while steering. If keys do nothing at all, the page may have lost focus: click the game area, then try again. Persistent problems are covered in troubleshooting.
