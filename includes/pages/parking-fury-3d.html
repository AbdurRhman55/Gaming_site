What the game is

Poki’s own description sets the scene: you drive muscle cars through the dark streets of a crowded city, steering around other vehicles and pedestrians, parking without damage — and, when the mission calls for it, taking a car you are not meant to have and hiding it. Missions pay out only if the car arrives unscratched.

It is listed under driving games by developer Brain Software, released March 2019 and last updated June 2022. Poki’s supported-devices field reads Desktop for this title, unlike several other parking games on the platform that list phones and tablets — so assume keyboard-and-mouse unless you have verified otherwise on your own device.

⚠ Do not confuse the two series. Parking Fury 1–3 are top-down games by Andriy Pidvirnyy on Coolmath Games. The 3D games are by Brain Software and live on Poki — different controls, different physics, different missions. See the original series guide if you wanted the top-down games.

FAQ

Parking Fury 3D questions

Is Parking Fury 3D the same as Parking Fury?
No — different developer, view and gameplay. The top-down trilogy is by Andriy Pidvirnyy on Coolmath Games; the 3D series is by Brain Software on Poki. The comparison table above lays it out.
Does Parking Fury 3D work on phones?
Poki’s supported-devices field for this game says Desktop only, and its controls assume a keyboard (arrow keys plus Ctrl). Other parking games on Poki do list phone and tablet support — see similar games for those.
How do you get in and out of cars?
Ctrl enters or exits vehicles in Parking Fury 3D. Bounty Hunter adds Space for the handbrake, and both sequels let you switch camera with C.
